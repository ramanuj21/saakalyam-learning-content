# Provenance

Preserve original identity, source work/consulted edition/locator, exact source revision and file/text digests where available. Source citation and copied/derived material are different: `sourceRefs` cite pinned references; `inputRefs` pin actual material inputs. Adaptations/transcriptions/transliterations need a method and exact inputs. Do not invent an edition, author, recording, syllabus mapping or source locator to fill a schema. Source pages remain external references until permissions are established. No legacy database or learner data is queried.
