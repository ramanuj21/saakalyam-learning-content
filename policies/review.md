# Review

Reviews identify exact target revision, SHA-256 of canonical file bytes, pinned reviewer identity, scope, decision, timestamp and critical dependencies. Configured authorities must be appointed by the owner. Self-review does not satisfy independent approval. Changed bytes or dependencies invalidate old review. Latest applicable decisions cannot be overridden by stale approved records. Subject correctness, source fidelity, rights/compatibility and pedagogical suitability remain human decisions; structural tests cannot supply them. No genuine approval is included initially.
