# Rights and licensing status

No blanket open licence is assigned by this initial implementation. The Phase 8.1 ODbL database / CC BY-SA authored content / separately decided software and schema licensing suggestions remain proposals requiring owner/legal decisions. This repository does not apply them to existing content by inference or alter the application licence.

Public source inspection and GitHub visibility are not blanket permission to redistribute or adapt third-party materials. Initial software, schemas, templates and newly authored documentation are published under the explicit repository-creation task; broader licensing terms remain pending. Do not describe the repository as an openly licensed Sanskrit dataset until the appropriate grants are adopted.

Every independently managed content record carries attribution and a rights reference. `basis: unknown` means no grant. Catalogue metadata, original material, particular representations, authored learning components and compiled database selection have different rights layers. A MediaAsset’s metadata rights do not clear its required reproduction-rights statement.

The two pilot references disclose short public-link facts and existing credits only. The PDF and recording are not bundled, downloaded by validation or presented as licensed. No source questions, answer keys, adapted lesson text, original PDFs, rendered slides or audio/video are published. Synthetic English test content establishes no Sanskrit, source or reviewer claim.
